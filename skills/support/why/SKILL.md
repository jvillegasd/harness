# Why

Answer from the record: read the commits and PR discussions that introduced the target and shaped its later changes. Search other available sources when the trail is thin. Cite what the authors wrote, mark your inferences, surface contradictions, and say what the record leaves unanswered.

Work inline by default. When an independent search has a named benefit, use `delegation` for a role that can access the source and verify its citations before synthesizing.
